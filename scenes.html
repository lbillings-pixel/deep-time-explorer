Scene workbench

Scenes drawn by code

Each picture below is assembled in your browser from separately drawn parts: sky, land or sea floor, water, plants and animals. Nothing here is a photograph or an AI image, and nothing is fetched from a server.

  • Ancient latitude sets the light: how high the sun stands, the color of the sky, the length of shadows.
  • Climate sets the palette. In the app, wet, dry or cold would come only from rock evidence.
  • The chapter and the scene type decide who may appear. A part is never drawn outside the chapters and scene types listed for it.
  • The place sets the view. The same place number always gives the same picture.

The parts gallery is at the foot of the page. Each group has its caveats behind a click: what is guessed, simplified or disputed.
